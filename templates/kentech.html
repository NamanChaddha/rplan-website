Pearl GTL Effluent Treatment Plant (C8)

Scope: EPC Systems

Directed the root Project Controls department (incorporating planning matrix optimization, structural variance paths, and rigorous contract control updates) for the **C8 portion of the Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP)** at Shell's landmark Pearl GTL complex.

The mandate encompassed complete oversight tracking from full-scale construction phases straight through mechanical testing, system hot-commissioning integrations, and technical handovers configured cleanly around highly tight pre-set system client schedules.

High-Diameter Firewater Piping Network

Directed full project control sequences covering the active construction, material verification, system testing, and hydraulic commissioning of a massive **20km firewater link pipeline infrastructure**.

The network successfully integrated mixed material layouts up to **60” maximum caliber diameters**, joining heavy-duty Carbon Steel (CS) headers with anti-corrosive Fiber-Reinforced Plastic (FRP) line extensions, primary plant monitors, system water sprinklers, and auxiliary high-pressure Jockey Pump system modules.
