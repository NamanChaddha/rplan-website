Late Shri Sitaram Chaddha

A Legacy of Pragmatic Genius & Entrepreneurial Pursuit

This page serves as a humble tribute to a visionary whose relentless drive, sharp intellect, and pragmatic approach to business laid the foundation for decades of success. His entrepreneurial spirit continues to guide us today.

The Foundation Stones

1990
M/S Immaculate Enterprises

The inaugural venture that sparked a decades-long entrepreneurial journey, establishing a benchmark for operational excellence and visionary leadership.

2004
M/S Tushar Trading Company

A flourishing enterprise that further cemented his reputation in the trading sector. This chapter was respectfully wound up following his passing, preserving its memory and integrity.

2011
M/s BREdutech

A testament to his forward-thinking mindset, expanding the group's horizons into the education sector. This venture embodies his commitment to the future by providing personalized education to empower students with the wings of education.

Carrying the Torch Forward

Guided by the enduring principles and pragmatic wisdom of Late Shri Sitaram Chaddha, The Chaddha Group continues to follow closely in his footsteps.

It is from this deep-rooted foundation of enterprise that rPlan took its formal birth in the year 2022. Honoring a legacy of rapid growth and strategic foresight, rPlan took off within a few short years, standing today as a proud continuation of a remarkable entrepreneurial journey.
