Project Site- Bu Hasa Valves replacement project for GASCO (NGL)

Headed the EPCM project for design, procurement, installation & commissioning of ESD valves and SA/DA actuators at GASCO’s NGL facility at Bu Hasa in UAE. Project Value around 7.5 Million USD.
Engineering - Completed by FEED Contractor - TEBODIN Bilfinger ME. Major decisions include

➢
TBE and selection of major valve and actuator vendors
➢
Site surveys and co-ordination for intricate fitment of components during replacement of 30year old valves with actuators
➢
DCS/ESD related E&I scopes as implemented by Honeywell
Major Procurement includes
➢
23 ESD Valves by MSA (Czech Rep).
➢
23 Double Acting pneumatic Actuators by Rotork (Germany)
➢
28 nos Single and Double acting quarter turn actuators by CAI (France)
➢
Factory acceptance and Site acceptance of all valves and actuators
Construction / Commissioning challenges include
➢
Shutdown management for replacement of actuators without changing valves during one day events and for replacement of 23 main ESD valves during the major shutdown of the plant
➢
Intricate rigging studies for replacements during major shutdown

Specific Achievement - Project achieved mechanical completion 6 months ahead of scheduled date and also completed within budget (15%). All Five shutdowns (Apr 15, May 15, Oct 15, Nov 15 & Apr 16) completed safely with planned rehabilitation of new CAI actuators (28nos) and new MSA Valves & ROTORK Actuators (23nos). Close to 20000 Inch Dia Plant LTCS/CS Piping (6” to 36”) revamped including Engineering, prefabrication, construction and commissioning as per Tebodin design during the course of reinstallation!
