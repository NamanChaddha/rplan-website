GASCO Bu Hasa Valves Replacement Project

Project Value: $7.5M USD

Headed the comprehensive EPC contract lifecycle covering the definitive design, international procurement, safety-critical installation, and final commissioning of high-pressure emergency shutdown (ESD) valves and specialized SA/DA actuators at GASCO's NGL Facility.

Engineering & Project Controls Management

  • Collaborated with FEED Contractor Tebodin Bilfinger ME for engineering layout clearances.
  • Conducted high-precision site surveys to plan the tight component fitment of modern hardware into a 30-year-old operational piping infrastructure.
  • Supervised integration coordinates for all DCS/ESD related E&I automated scopes executed by Honeywell.

Construction & Shutdown Challenges

Executed a rigorous shutdown management strategy requiring intricate rigging studies. Completed actuator hot-swaps seamlessly without changing active valves during compressed 1-day windows, and replaced all 23 massive main ESD valves during the plant's primary turnaround.

Project Outcome: Achieved final mechanical completion 6 months ahead of schedule while managing costs aggressively to close the project 15% under budget.

H2S Gas Removal Facility Initiation (ICAD)

Project Value: $10M USD

Concurrently managed the formal initiation and early setup stages for the deployment of a new H2S removal facility inside the existing Central Receiving Station (CRS) at ICAD near Mussaffah (April 2016 – October 2016).

Approvals & Engineering

Launched initial engineering with sub-contractor WG Wood Group Kenny. Orchestrated environmental risk studies to successfully secure a critical De Minimis status report from ADNOC alongside geotechnical and topographical field surveys.

Procurement Launch

Headed initial Technical Bid Evaluations (TBE) for high-lead machinery and supplies, including catalyst orders from Johnson Matthey and Scrubber Vessels from Descon-Sharjah, before successfully handing over the organized framework to the incoming permanent PM team.
